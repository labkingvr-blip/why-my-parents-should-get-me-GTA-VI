🎮 What is GTA VI?

Grand Theft Auto VI is an action-adventure video game developed by Rockstar Games. It takes place in the fictional state of Leonida, including the city of Vice City.

The game features an open world, a storyline, exploration and two main characters: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval.

⭐ Why I'm interested

  • The detailed open world and environments.
  • The storyline and characters.
  • The graphics and technology behind the game.
  • Exploring the fictional world and discovering its features.
  • Being able to enjoy a major game release.

I'm interested in the game as entertainment, and I understand that its fictional content is not something to copy in real life.

🔞 The age rating — an important point

GTA VI is rated PEGI 18. It is intended for adults and is not suitable for under-18s.

The rating includes strong violence, sexual themes and the use of illegal drugs. I understand why this could concern you, and I don't expect you to ignore the rating.

You can read the official information here: Official PEGI GTA VI rating .
